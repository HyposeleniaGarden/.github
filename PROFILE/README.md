### About Us

This organization hosts the custom server-side mods, event engines, and infrastructure configs powering our private SMP. Built primarily around lightweight Fabric environments with proximity voice chat, Discord integration, and custom narrative events.

---

### Repositories & Custom Mods

- **`solenne` (*The Crimson Fountain of Villa Solenne*)** — Mod to facilitate our murder-mystery server event featuring interactive mannequins, NPC pathing, spatial clue progression, dialog voting, custom music/SFX triggers, and an optional client companion for operator editor overlays and HUDs.
- **`ShoreBridger`** — Minecraft <> Discord chat bridge powered by JDA and webhooks.
- **`ShoreStylish`** — Chat and nickname formatting utility.
- **`Mod List`** — Add `/mods` and `/modlist` to view loaded mods, nested JIJ dependencies, and enabled datapacks.
